Product

One architecture. Modules you turn on.

BladeOS is the commercial white-label edition of the club operating system: a public club site, club operations, a member portal, a kiosk, and a schedule screen. The demo opens all of them on an empty database.

Surfaces

Five places, one club record.

Staff

Club operations

Dashboard, schedule, members, billing, coaches, and the add-on modules. Midnight navigation, light work surface.

Athletes

Member portal

Profile, household, membership, bookings, private lessons, invoices, competitions, and video library.

Public

Club website

The academy name, schedule, and memberships. BladeOS stays in a discreet line.

Front desk

Kiosk check-in

Self-service arrival. A tap writes attendance. No member list until the club creates one.

Lobby

Schedule screen

A dark board for the room: class, weapon, and time. Empty until a class is published.

Data

A new club does not inherit another club.

Provisioning creates the module tables and leaves them empty. Plans, coaches, classes, invoices, leads, and videos are entered by that club. The demo uses the same rule, stored only in the browser.

Protected across every club

  • Navigation, spacing, and component behavior
  • Empty, loading, and error states
  • Success, warning, and error colors
  • One database boundary per club
